The project develops robust formation controllers under plant uncertainty and external disturbance. It currently includes validated controller-design scripts and short, disposable satellite-model smoke tests, alongside carefully separated ROS 2 hardware experiments.

The next research stage is to complete safe TurtleBot3 multi-agent implementation, compare simulation and hardware error metrics, and incorporate the experimental evidence into the evolving manuscript.
