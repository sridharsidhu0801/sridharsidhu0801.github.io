This research studies how relative-position-dependent coupling changes disturbance propagation in long formations. The objective is to characterize when local interactions produce string-stable or mesh-stable collective behavior as the number of agents grows.

## Engineering contribution

- Developed higher-order multi-agent models and stability-analysis workflows.
- Compared theoretical gain conditions with numerical frequency- and time-domain results.
- Organized the evolving derivations, MATLAB experiments, figures, and manuscript lineage into a reproducible research repository.
- Preserved historical variants while separating them from the current scientific baseline.

The work remains under active theoretical and numerical development; unpublished repository material is intentionally not linked publicly.
