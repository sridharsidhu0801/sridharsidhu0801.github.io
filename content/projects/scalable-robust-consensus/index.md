This research studies how local graph sparsity and node-degree bounds can support scalable stability and robustness guarantees in large multi-agent networks.

The validation suite combines deterministic trials, randomized graph experiments, worst-case sampling, gain analysis, and solver-based feasibility studies. Eleven bounded MATLAB workflows have been reproduced successfully; deliberately expensive parameter sweeps remain documented rather than being presented as completed results.
