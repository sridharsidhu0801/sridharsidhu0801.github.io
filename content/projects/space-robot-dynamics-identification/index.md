This project investigated the coupled dynamics of a robotic manipulator mounted on a free-moving base. The experimental workflow used a physical testbed and ROS-based data collection to identify dynamic parameters and analyze base motion generated by manipulator movement.

The work also explored a reaction-wheel concept for reducing platform yaw error. It strengthened my experience in system identification, robot dynamics, experimental setup, and the transition from a mathematical model to measured hardware behavior.
