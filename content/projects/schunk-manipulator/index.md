This project developed motion and control workflows for a seven-degree-of-freedom SCHUNK LWA4D manipulator. Work progressed from kinematic analysis and simulated motion in Gazebo to communication and control of the physical arm through ROS-Industrial and MoveIt.

The preserved project also includes the vendor and CAN-USB materials needed to understand the original integration environment, clearly separated from authored implementation work.
