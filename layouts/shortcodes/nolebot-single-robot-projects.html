Path-Tracking Controllers

Controller development and experimental tracking for three reference geometries: a straight line, a circular path, and a figure-eight path.

  • Reference-path generation
  • Heading and position-error regulation
  • Trajectory-specific tuning and evaluation

02

DDWMR Modeling

Modeling of the differential-drive wheeled mobile robot as the foundation for controller design, simulation, and hardware implementation.

  • Wheel-speed to body-motion relationships
  • Planar pose and heading dynamics
  • Simulation-to-hardware parameter workflow
Map and navigation media will be added
03

SLAM and Autonomous Navigation

Mapping the laboratory, localizing the robot, and navigating from a start pose to a goal pose with layered obstacle avoidance.

  • SLAM-based laboratory map generation
  • Global planning around stationary obstacles
  • Local planning around moving obstacles
Teleoperation media will be added
04

Single-NoleBot Teleoperation

Remote human operation of one NoleBot through a command-and-feedback interface for platform testing and controlled driving experiments.

  • Operator command interface
  • Velocity-command delivery
  • Robot feedback and safe platform operation
