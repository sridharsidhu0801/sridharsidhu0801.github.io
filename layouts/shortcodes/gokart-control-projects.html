Vehicle Control and Hardware Integration

01 · MODELING

Vehicle Dynamics and Steering Control

I developed a vehicle dynamics model in MATLAB/Simulink and evaluated Stanley, PID, and LQR steering controllers. The project also includes nonlinear modeling and NMPC simulation work.

These studies connect path tracking and controller development with the constraints of the physical vehicle.

02 · EMBEDDED CONTROL

Steering Angle and Rate Regulation

I implemented steering PID control in Embedded C on an Arduino and extended the logic to regulate steering rate for smoother, more responsive actuation.

A startup routine sweeps the left and right travel limits to establish the steering reference instead of assuming the initial wheel position is zero.

03 · DRIVE-BY-WIRE

Longitudinal PI Cruise Control

I developed the PI cruise-control logic on the Jetson Orin Nano, using motor-encoder feedback from the VESC.

The cruise loop computes motor commands from measured and desired speed and sends them to the VESC for actuation.

04 · COMMAND SAFETY

Watchdog for Missing Commands

During physical testing, the motor continued running when ROS 2 commands stopped arriving because the last received input remained active.

I introduced a watchdog node to detect missing commands and prevent continued actuation from stale inputs.

05 · FEEDBACK TIMING

Fresh Measurements for Control

I identified a mismatch between command publishing and encoder-feedback frequencies. New commands were sometimes computed from outdated measurements, producing erratic responses.

I kept feedback updates faster than the control-command frequency so the controller could operate on more recent measurements.

06 · MECHANICAL REDESIGN

Steering Torque and Transmission

Repeated coupler breakage indicated excessive torsional loading. I analyzed worst-case steering torque using vehicle mass, linkage geometry, and tire turning and rolling resistance.

I redesigned the assembly with sprocket-and-chain transmission and a 60:1 gearbox, resolving repeated coupler failures and improving reliability.
